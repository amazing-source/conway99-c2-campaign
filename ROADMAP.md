# Roadmap

The campaign is now intentionally reduced to three lanes.

## Lane A · one conceptual inventor

Objective: discover a genuinely global compatibility language or a constructive grammar for Theorem C.

Hard rule:

> If the next step is merely “inspect a larger neighborhood,” stop and mutate the representation instead.

The inventor should work from the native four-state kernel and the failure ledger, not from the entire historical repository.

## Lane B · Route C

Run the finite 156-envelope computation in parallel as a certified NOT-EX backstop.

Immediate task: reconcile the reported `91/156` count and the estimated `~6600 CPU-hours` against the authoritative tracker.

## Lane C · native solver

Build the specialized 861-variable, four-state exact solver.

Immediate milestone: propagation benchmark, not a week-long blind run.

## Do not proliferate threads

Avoid opening many undifferentiated “solve Conway” sessions.

Every research session should have one of:

- representation invention;
- constructive extension;
- global destruction;
- exact computation / certification.

## Positive endpoint

One exact `(Q,N)` → reconstruct → exact 99-vertex verification.

## Negative endpoint

Universal contradiction or exhaustive certified finite elimination.

## Asymmetric remainder

Even a completed C2 nonexistence result does **not** settle the existence of an asymmetric Conway 99-graph.
