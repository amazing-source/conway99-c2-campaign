# AI research campaign

## Objective

Use multiple independent model trajectories without allowing them to collapse immediately into the same familiar mathematical basins.

## Generation 1 · Constructor vs Destroyer

### Constructor mandate

Try to build one exact `(Q,N)`.

Observed behavior:

- concentrated on symmetry ansätze and generic search;
- excluded several symmetry-restricted families;
- derived a small stabilizer lemma;
- importantly, generic constructive heuristics also failed on the known positive `m=11` BvLS control.

Conclusion:

Search failure at `m=7` is not evidence for nonexistence.

### Destroyer mandate

Assume a complete solution and derive a global contradiction.

Observed behavior:

- drifted to a Hodge-Laplacian route;
- independently recovered the edge-Laplacian lower bound and rational `H1=0`;
- targeted duplication check showed this is already the old C1 Hodge route, which had been explicitly closed as a reformulation without leverage.

Conclusion:

Opposite role prompts alone did not prevent convergence into familiar attractor basins.

No Generation-1 cross-pollination was executed before this duplication was recognized.

## Representation-invention phase

The campaign then hid matrix language and supplied the problem as a native 42-label, four-state system.

The research task became:

> What is the problem actually made of?

This produced port graphs, coordinate link pairings, debt ledgers, two-faced boards, and response cards.

Most were eventually killed, but the process supplied sharper route-closure theorems and exposed how quickly apparently compressed descriptions recover full label identity.

## Cross-pollination policy

Blind phase first.

After independent return, exchange only concrete artifacts:

- exact theorem;
- exact counterexample;
- explicit partial object;
- exact computational artifact.

Do not exchange speculative interpretation.

## Innovation lesson

The repeated failure is not lack of deductive competence. It is convergence of exploratory search toward high-probability mathematical basins:

- matrix → spectrum/Hodge/lattice;
- finite constraints → SAT;
- symmetry → orbit ansatz;
- local obstruction → larger neighborhood.

The campaign therefore treats **representation search** as an explicit research problem.
